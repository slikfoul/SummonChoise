# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Choose Dead Raiser skeleton equipment: bow, sword, sword and shield, or the game's random selection.
- Choose a Spirit Caller species or use Missing mode to fill spirit slots in rounds as the staff is upgraded.
- Adjust how far from you your Spirit Caller spirits stop while following outside combat.
- See separate status icons with active counts for your skeletons and spirits.

## 0.4.0

- Set how close your Spirit Caller spirits stop when following you outside combat.

## 0.3.0

- Missing mode fills additional rounds of spirits as the Spirit Caller is upgraded.

## 0.2.0

- Choose a specific Spirit Caller spirit, retain vanilla random selection, or summon missing spirit types without duplicates.
- Show a separate Spirit Caller status icon with the active spirit count below it.

## 0.1.2

- Renamed to Summon Choise.

## 0.1.1

- The summon indicator follows the game's active creature list and summon-distance rules.

## 0.1.0

- A status icon shows the number of your active summoned skeletons and disappears when none remain.
- Summoner identification keeps other players' skeletons out of your count, including when using random equipment.

## 0.0.3

- Archer selection produces bow-only skeletons without shields.

## 0.0.2

- Persistent skeleton type selection through Configuration Manager.

## 0.0.1

- Persistent selection of archers, sword warriors, shield warriors, or random Dead Raiser skeletons.
- Equipment selection through the game's synchronized seed and native equipment categories.
