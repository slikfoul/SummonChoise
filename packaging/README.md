# Summon Choise

Choose what Dead Raiser and Spirit Caller summon. See how many of your summons are active, and give your spirits more room when they follow you.

## Dead Raiser

In **Configuration Manager → Summon Choise**, set **Skeleton type** to `Random`, `Archer` (bow), `Warrior` (sword), or `ShieldWarrior` (sword and shield). The choice applies to new summons. `Random` is the default.

## Spirit Caller

- **Spirit Caller type** — choose Bear, Moose, Wolf, or Boar, or use `Random` for the game's selection.
- **Missing** (default) — fills the roster in rounds: bear → moose → wolf → boar. It prioritizes the type you have the fewest of. A quality 2 staff can fill two rounds; a quality 3 staff can fill three. The game's summon limits still apply.
- **Spirit follow distance** — sets where your spirits stop while following you outside combat. The default is 6 m. Choose 3–10 m; 3 m restores the game's usual distance. The setting applies while your client controls their movement.

## Summon counters

**Show summon buff** and **Show spirit buff** display separate icons for your skeletons and spirits. The number under each icon counts your active summons in the loaded world. Each icon hides at zero. Summons created before their staff was supported by the mod are not counted.

## Installation

Install on the client that casts the spells. The mod uses the game's existing creatures and summon limits.
